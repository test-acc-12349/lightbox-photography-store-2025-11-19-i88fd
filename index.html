About Lightbox

Our story and commitment to photography excellence

Our Heritage

Founded in 2010, Lightbox Photography Store emerged from a passion for democratizing professional photography. Our founders, a collective of award-winning photographers and tech enthusiasts, recognized a gap in the market for a comprehensive platform that combines expert guidance with beautiful gallery solutions. What started as a small community has grown into a trusted resource serving thousands of photographers worldwide, from hobbyists capturing their first images to professionals running successful photography businesses.

Our Mission

We are committed to empowering photographers at every level by providing exceptional tools, expert knowledge, and a supportive community. Our mission is to make professional-quality photography accessible to everyone, regardless of their experience or budget. We believe that great photography comes from the combination of the right tools, proper guidance, and continuous learning. Through our platform, we strive to inspire creativity, foster collaboration, and help photographers achieve their artistic and business goals while maintaining the highest standards of customer service and product excellence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Lightbox

What makes Lightbox different from other photography platforms?

Lightbox stands out by combining three essential services in one platform: professional gallery solutions, expert equipment guidance, and a vibrant community. Unlike other platforms that focus solely on storage or social sharing, we provide end-to-end support for photographers. Our expert team offers personalized equipment recommendations, our gallery tools are specifically designed for professional presentation, and our community connects photographers for collaboration and learning. This integrated approach means you get everything you need to succeed in one place, with dedicated support from photography professionals who understand your challenges.

How do I get expert equipment guidance?

Our expert consultation is simple and accessible. You can schedule a one-on-one consultation with our photography experts through your dashboard, participate in live Q&A sessions held twice weekly, or browse our extensive equipment recommendation database filtered by your photography style and budget. For premium members, we offer unlimited consultations and priority support. Simply describe your photography goals, current equipment, and budget, and our team will provide tailored recommendations based on your specific needs. We also provide detailed comparison guides and hands-on reviews of popular equipment to help you make informed decisions.

Is my photography portfolio secure on Lightbox?

Absolutely. We take security and privacy extremely seriously. All images are stored with bank-level encryption, and we maintain regular security audits and backups. You have complete control over who can access your portfolio with customizable privacy settings, password protection options, and the ability to watermark your images. We also provide detailed access logs so you can see exactly who has viewed your work. Your intellectual property is protected with our Digital Rights Management (DRM) system, and we comply with all international data protection regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

What subscription plans are available?

We offer flexible subscription plans to suit every photographer's needs. Our Starter plan ($9.99/month) includes basic gallery hosting and community access. The Professional plan ($29.99/month) adds unlimited galleries, expert consultations, and advanced customization. Our Premium plan ($79.99/month) includes everything plus priority support, advanced analytics, and exclusive networking events. We also offer annual subscriptions with significant discounts (up to 30% off) and a 30-day free trial so you can experience all features risk-free. All plans include our satisfaction guarantee—if you're not happy, we'll refund your money within 60 days.
